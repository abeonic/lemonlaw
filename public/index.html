Florida’s Lemon Law, in plain English

Florida’s Motor Vehicle Warranty Enforcement Act can protect buyers and lessees of new vehicles with a substantial defect.

24 months

Report the defect

Report a defect that substantially impairs the vehicle’s use, value, or safety to the manufacturer or its authorized dealer within 24 months of delivery.

3 attempts

Track repeat repairs

After three repair attempts for the same defect, written notice to the manufacturer generally gives it one final opportunity to repair the vehicle.

15 days

Count time in the shop

At 15 cumulative days out of service for qualifying repairs, notify the manufacturer in writing. Different requirements apply to a claim based on total time out of service.
